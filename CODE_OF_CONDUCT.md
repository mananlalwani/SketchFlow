# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

For a public release, maintainers must publish a monitored conduct contact in the repository settings. Reports should not contain unnecessary personal data and will be handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove or reject contributions, comments, or participants that violate this policy.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
